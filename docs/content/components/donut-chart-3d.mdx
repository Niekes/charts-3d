The pie of this registry, with a hole: a slice is as wide as its share of the
whole and as tall as its value, relative to the largest, so the depth carries
the value twice over - the angle tells the share, the height compares the
slices where the tilt bends their angles. A pie whose depth is only thickness
has no place here ([ADR-0014](https://github.com/Niekes/charts-3d/blob/main/docs/adr/0014-depth-shows-data.md)).
The slices run in the order of the data, clockwise seen from above, the first
from the back; each takes the color its `id` keys in the chart config, as the
slices of the shadcn/ui pie do, unless `series` names another field.

## Enter and leave

New data may also bring and drop whole slices - a slice follows the name of
its entry, in the `id` field, within its series. An added browser widens out
of its neighbours and rises as it fades in; a removed one narrows back into
them and sinks as it fades out, the mirror of its entering, where it stood in
the ring (see Enter in the
[glossary](https://github.com/Niekes/charts-3d/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md)). A
value that drops to 0 leaves the same way, and one that rises from 0 comes. A
slice that changes its place in the order swaps with the ones it passes
halfway through the glide: a ring has no room to let it by.

## Many marks

A thousand slices - the most populous places of the world, the capitals in a
color of their own - and the chart stays interactive. The scene is one list of
marks, so the demo starts on `"canvas"`, the renderer for many marks, and the
select shows the same scene on SVG.
