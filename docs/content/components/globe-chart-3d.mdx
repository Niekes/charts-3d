The globe is a grid of tiles between meridians and parallels, `step` degrees
wide. An entry names a place, in the `lon` and `lat` fields, and the tile that
holds it rises to the entry's `value`, in the color of its `series`; a tile
without an entry lies flat in the wall color. Aggregation happens before the
chart: round a list of places to the grid, or hand the chart a grid it already
has, one entry per tile.

## Update

Press Update: every tile glides to its new value over the `animationDuration`
of its container, and the ceiling - the height of the tallest tile - glides
along, so no tile jumps.

## Enter and leave

New data may also bring and drop tiles. An added event grows out of the globe
and fades in; a removed one shrinks back into it and fades out, the mirror
of its entering (see Enter in the
[glossary](https://github.com/Niekes/charts-3d/blob/master/GLOSSARY.md)).
Here each event is a tile of its type, `series`, and its strength the height.

## Renderers

The same chart on every renderer, side by side. The marks are data the chart hands to the container, and `"svg"`, `"canvas"` and `"webgl"` draw them all - where the browser has the requested renderer, `"webgl"` falling back to `"canvas"`, and where it has none, to SVG.

## Many marks

Tiles of 3°, seven thousand of them and thousands rising - and the chart
stays interactive. The scene is one list of marks, so the demo starts on
`"canvas"`, the renderer for many marks, and the select shows the same scene
on SVG and WebGL.
