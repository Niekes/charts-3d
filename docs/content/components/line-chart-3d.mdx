The entries of `data` are points in space, joined in the order of the data:
a trajectory, a spiral, a walk. `series` names the field that tells the lines
apart. Pointing at a line lights the point nearest the pointer, with a dot
on it, and the tooltip reads that point. For entries along x with a value per series - the
line chart of a spreadsheet - see the [area chart](/docs/components/area-chart-3d).

## Enter and leave

With an `id` field, a point follows its name through updates: a new one
unfolds from the point before it on its line - from the one after it, at the
start - as it fades in, a gone one folds back into it - the mirror of its
entering - and one that moves glides (see Enter in the
[glossary](https://github.com/Niekes/charts-3d/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md)). So a
line grows at its end as the data gains a point, and shrinks from its start
as it loses one. Without an `id`, points are named by their position in
`data`, which animates appending and removing at the end. A line that is all
new stands in place and fades in. Data may come faster than the line glides,
as from a feed: every point goes on from where it is, a new one starting on
the point before it wherever that has come to.

## Many marks

The Lorenz attractor, three thousand points of it - and the chart stays
interactive. The scene is one list of marks, so the demo starts on
`"canvas"`, the renderer for many marks, and the select shows the same scene
on SVG. `colors` names two config keys, and each point's part of the line
takes the color of its height on the ramp between them, in place of the
color of its series.

## Gaps

An entry whose `x`, `y` or `z` is missing or not a number has no point, and
its line stops before it and starts again after it: no segment bridges a
value the data does not have. A point alone between two gaps is a circle as wide as the line.
