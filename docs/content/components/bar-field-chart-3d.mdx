## Data

The chart draws what the data says and decides nothing of its own:
`data[row][column]` is the value of one bar. Its row and column place it -
the columns along x, the rows along z, at the values `domain` names - the
cell of the grid gives its footprint, its value its height from 0, and the
ramp between the two `colors` at the lowest and the highest value its color.
A value that is not a finite number draws no bar. The gap between two bars
is fixed, a fifth of the cell, so neighbours stay apart.

## Enter and leave

The grid may grow and shrink, while every bar keeps its value. A new bar
grows from the floor as it fades in, while the axes make room; a gone one
shrinks back into it as it fades out, the mirror of its entering (see Enter
in the [glossary](https://github.com/Niekes/charts-3d/blob/master/GLOSSARY.md)).
