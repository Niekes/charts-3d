## Enter and leave

New data may also bring, drop and reorder whole rows - a bar follows the name
of its entry, in the `index` field. An added month grows out of the floor
and fades in while the row axis makes room, its line growing to the new
tick, its label fading in; a removed one shrinks back into the floor and
fades out, the mirror of its entering, while the rows around it glide to
their new cells and the axis closes the gap (see Enter in the
[glossary](https://github.com/Niekes/charts-3d/blob/master/GLOSSARY.md)).
