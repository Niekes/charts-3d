## Enter and leave

With an `id` field, a point follows its name through updates: a new one grows
out of nothing at its place, a gone one shrinks away where it stands, and one
that moves glides - the mirror of its entering (see Enter in the
[glossary](https://github.com/Niekes/charts-3d/blob/master/GLOSSARY.md)).
Without an `id`, points are named by their position in `data`, which animates
appending and removing at the end.

## Renderers

The same chart on every renderer, side by side. The marks are data the chart hands to the container, and `"svg"`, `"canvas"` and `"webgl"` draw them all - where the browser has the requested renderer, `"webgl"` falling back to `"canvas"`, and where it has none, to SVG.

## Many marks

Five thousand points - and the chart stays interactive. The scene is one list
of marks, so the demo starts on `"canvas"`, the renderer for many marks, and
the select shows the same scene on SVG and WebGL.
